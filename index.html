Tag it right

Every address remembers its own network.

Solana, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Bitcoin and more, each entry is tagged with its network and asset, so you never send USDC down the wrong chain by mistake.

Lock it down

Turn on a password, keep it hidden.

Set a password in Settings and the panel stays locked until you unlock it, with an auto-lock after an hour of inactivity. The check runs entirely on your device. Nothing is ever sent anywhere.

It protects itself

Saved wallets seal themselves.

The moment an address is written, the row locks. Editing it again takes a deliberate unlock step, so a stray click or keystroke can never quietly change where your money goes.
